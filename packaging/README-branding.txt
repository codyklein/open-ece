OpenECE approved branding

The Waveform O geometry and its 16/20/24 px optical variants are frozen.
Primary cobalt: #2854C5. Dark-background blue: #96AEF2.
Light lockup: cobalt icon with #1A2738 wordmark.
Dark lockup: light-blue icon with white wordmark.
Black and white monochrome icons remain official alternatives.

openece.ico has ten approved frames: 16/20/24/32/40/48/64/96/128/256 px.
png/ contains the primary transparent icons through 1024 px. Preserve padding;
do not crop, distort, recolor, or substitute the small-size artwork.
SVGs are self-contained and the Noto Sans SemiBold wordmark is outlined.
No font software is included or needed. The Noto Sans OFL attribution is in
../licenses/Noto-Sans/Noto-Sans-OFL.txt. Original artwork is under OpenECE's
MIT license, ../LICENSE. See the source assets/branding/README.md for provenance
and the controlled export procedure.

The portable package contains useful exports, not the export/development tools.
It does not install file associations, a splash screen, an updater or an installer.
